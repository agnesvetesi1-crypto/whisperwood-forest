WHISPERWOOD FOREST — EPISODE 3: "BENNY AND THE BIG STORM" ("Benny és a Nagy Zivatar")
Pixar/Disney 3D animation style throughout, 16:9, warm consistent color palette. Characters: Benny (small gray rabbit, anxious), Bruno (large brown bear, warm and steady). Lesson: courage isn't not being scared — it's staying anyway, and it's easier together. No scares beyond mild storm/thunder (no real danger), ages 3-7 audience.

SCENE 1 (0:00): Establishing shot of Whisperwood Forest in the late afternoon, sky shifting from gold to a heavy grey-blue, wind picking up and bending the treetops, leaves swirling off branches, distant thunder rumbling low. Warm but uneasy mood. Narrator: "Some afternoons in Whisperwood Forest turn without warning. Today, the wind had a different kind of whisper — one that meant a storm was coming."

SCENE 2 (0:15): Benny hops along a mossy path, ears twitching, then suddenly freezes as a gust of wind rustles hard through the bushes around him, looking up nervously at the darkening sky, speaking to himself: "Oh no. Oh no no no. That sky does NOT look friendly."

SCENE 3 (0:30): A low rumble of thunder rolls across the sky. Benny's whole body flinches, ears pinning back flat, and he bolts across the clearing toward his burrow, calling out as he runs: "Nope! Nope, I am OUT, see you after the storm, bye!"

SCENE 4 (0:45): Inside Benny's cozy burrow, dim and root-lined. Benny is curled into the smallest possible ball in the corner, ears pressed down over his head, eyes squeezed shut, trembling slightly as muffled thunder rumbles outside. Narrator: "Deep underground, Benny was safe. But safe didn't feel very brave. It just felt very, very alone."

SCENE 5 (1:00): Bruno lumbers calmly through the forest as the first fat raindrops begin to fall, humming to himself, then slows and glances toward Benny's usual spot near the big oak, noticing it's empty, speaking to himself: "Hm. No Benny by the oak today. Wonder where that little guy got to."

SCENE 6 (1:15): Bruno crouches down at the small entrance of Benny's burrow, rain dripping off his fur, peering in gently, speaking softly: "Benny? It's just me. Mind if a big wet bear squeezes in?" Benny, heard from inside, small and shaky: "I— I'm okay. I just don't like the loud parts."

SCENE 7 (1:30): Bruno squeezes into the burrow entrance beside Benny, settling down warm and close, speaking gently: "It's just noise, Benny, that's all thund—" A HUGE crack of thunder booms directly overhead. Bruno's whole massive body flinches hard, ears back, eyes wide, cutting himself off mid-word.

SCENE 8 (1:45): Benny blinks up at Bruno, surprised, having clearly seen him flinch, speaking with wide eyes: "Wait... are YOU scared too?" Bruno rubs the back of his neck sheepishly: "...A little. Every single time. Never really goes away."

SCENE 9 (2:00): Bruno and Benny sit close together in the burrow entrance, rain falling steadily beyond it. Bruno, speaking warmly: "Being big doesn't make the scared part go away, Benny. It just means I've had more practice sitting with it." Benny listens, ears slowly relaxing.

SCENE 10 (2:15): A bright flash of lightning lights up the burrow entrance, followed instantly by a sharp crack of thunder. Both Benny and Bruno flinch hard at the exact same moment, then catch each other's eye, Benny bursts into giggles: "Okay that one got you too, I SAW it!" Bruno chuckles: "Caught red-pawed."

SCENE 11 (2:30): Benny and Bruno sit huddled together in the burrow entrance, Benny leaning into Bruno's warm fur, both watching the rain fall, calm now despite the ongoing storm outside. Narrator: "So they sat, and they waited, and every time the sky rumbled, they flinched together instead of alone. Somehow, that made all the difference."

SCENE 12 (2:45): The rain outside the burrow entrance softens to a light drizzle, then stops. Sunlight breaks weakly through the clearing clouds. Benny peeks his head out first, blinking in surprise at the calm, speaking: "Hey... hey, it's over! We made it!"

SCENE 13 (3:00): Benny and Bruno step out of the burrow together into the rain-washed forest, puddles sparkling in the returning sunlight, droplets glittering on every leaf, a faint rainbow arcing over the trees. Narrator: "Benny learned something that day that even Bruno needed reminding of sometimes: being brave was never about not being scared. It was about staying — and it was always a little easier together."

SCENE 14 (3:15): Benny stands in the sparkling, rain-washed clearing, turning to face the camera, warm and happy, Bruno visible beside him smiling proudly: "There's always something new happening here in Whisperwood Forest — come back soon and see what we get up to next!"

SCENE 15 (3:30): Coco stands facing the camera directly in a warm, sunny spot, tail wagging happily, big friendly smile, warm inviting light: "If you had fun with us today, ask a grown-up to help you hit that subscribe button, so you never miss our next adventure here in Whisperwood Forest!"

Continuous score arc: uneasy/anticipatory as the storm builds (scenes 1-4), tense but warm during Bruno's search and the reveal (5-9), a lighter comedic lift for the flinch-and-laugh beat (10), settling to calm and tender while they wait it out (11), then bright and warm for the resolution and close (12-14), staying warm and bright through the subscribe CTA (15).
