WHISPERWOOD FOREST — EPISODE 0: "MEET THE FRIENDS OF WHISPERWOOD FOREST"
Pixar/Disney 3D animation style throughout, 16:9, warm consistent color palette. Introduces all 8 characters: Coco (fox), Oliver (owl), Benny (bunny), Bruno (bear), Luna (wolf), Hazel (deer), Milo (hedgehog), Pip (squirrel). No scares, no real danger — only warmth and gentle personality moments, ages 3-7 audience. Ambient forest sound throughout, no background music unless noted.

SCENE 1 (0:00): Establishing shot of magical Whisperwood Forest at golden morning hour. Camera glides slowly through tall trees, sunlight streaming through leaves, colorful wildflowers, a sparkling stream, birds and butterflies drifting through the air. Peaceful, warm, inviting. No characters yet. Narrator: "Welcome to Whisperwood Forest — a magical place where the sun always finds a way through the leaves, and where eight very good friends call home. Today, we'd like you to meet them all."

SCENE 2 (0:15): Wide shot of a sunny forest clearing. In the distance, eight small silhouetted animal figures gather and wave to each other excitedly under the golden light. Camera slowly pushes forward toward them. Narrator: "Ready? Let's start with the one who's probably already running toward you."

SCENE 3 (0:30): Coco bounds energetically into frame through the sunlit forest, skids to a stop, spins once with excitement, big grin, tail wagging fast, facing camera: "Hi! Hi hi hi! My name's Coco — I'm a fox, in case the tail and the ears didn't already give it away!"

SCENE 4 (0:45): Coco talking animatedly, gesturing with her paws, eyes wide with enthusiasm, standing among flowers: "I love finding new things — bugs, berries, butterflies, weird noises coming from the bushes, all of it! If something exciting is happening anywhere in this forest, I'm probably already there — usually before I've finished thinking it through."

SCENE 5 (1:00): Coco's ears perk up suddenly at a rustling sound offscreen, eyes wide with delight: "Ooh, wait — did you hear that?! I have to go check — nice meeting you, bye!" — dashes off into the bushes mid-sentence, playful energetic motion.

SCENE 6 (1:15): Oliver the owl perched calmly on a low branch where Coco just disappeared, adjusting his round glasses, turning to camera, warm dappled light: "That was Coco. And I'm Oliver — I've lived in this forest a long time, and I've learned that most problems get smaller once you stop and actually think about them."

SCENE 7 (1:30): Oliver tilts his head thoughtfully, wings folded, wise gentle expression, warm afternoon forest light: "Whenever Coco needs... gentle redirecting, I'm usually not far away. Someone has to ask the second question before everyone runs off after the first exciting thing."

SCENE 8 (1:45): Oliver, still perched, speaks calmly: "Now — let's go find someone who could use a bit of quiet company." Then spreads his wings and glides gracefully off his branch, soaring low over the treetops, golden light through canopy.

SCENE 9 (2:00): Benny the bunny peeks out shyly from behind a large flower, ears twitching nervously, small and gray, big cautious eyes, soft dappled light: "Um. Hello. I'm... I'm Benny. I'm a bunny. I'm a little bit shy — you probably already noticed that."

SCENE 10 (2:15): Benny slowly steps out fully from behind the flower, still nervous but standing a little taller, ears slowly relaxing: "My friends say I'm braver than I think I am. I'm... still not totally sure about that. But I'm working on it. Every day, a little bit."

SCENE 11 (2:30): Benny takes a deep breath, closes his eyes, then hops forward bravely over a small log he was clearly nervous about, landing safely and opening his eyes with surprised pride, warm encouraging light: "See? A little bit braver already."

SCENE 12 (2:45): Bruno the bear lumbers warmly into frame, big and friendly, holding a berry between two claws, warm chuckle, golden forest light: "Name's Bruno. I'm a bear, in case that wasn't obvious. Big, a little slow, hungry pretty much all the time."

SCENE 13 (3:00): Bruno sits down heavily but gently on a soft patch of moss, offering the berry outward toward camera with a warm smile: "But I give the best hugs in this whole forest — ask anybody. Well. Ask Benny. Gently. He startles easy."

SCENE 14 (3:15): Bruno spots Benny nearby, opens his arms wide for a gentle hug: "See? Perfectly safe." Benny hesitates, then steps into the embrace happily: "Okay, this part I like." Warm heartwarming light, each speaks only during his own line.

SCENE 15 (3:30): Luna the wolf stands tall and calm at the edge of the clearing, alert posture, gray fur catching the light, scanning the forest protectively before her expression softens, facing camera: "I'm Luna. Wolf. I keep an eye on everyone out here."

SCENE 16 (3:45): Luna walks calmly along a ridge overlooking the forest, watching over the distant clearing where the other friends are gathered, warm late-afternoon light: "Not because they need it, exactly. Just because that's what friends do for each other. If you're ever lost in Whisperwood Forest, just call out. I'll hear you."

SCENE 17 (4:00): Luna's ears perk toward a faint sound in the distance, turns her head sharply, then relaxes into a small warm smile toward camera: "Come on. I think you'll like the next one."

SCENE 18 (4:15): Hazel the deer twirls gracefully through tall grass in a sunlit meadow, petals drifting around her, elegant and light on her feet, dreamy golden light: "I'm Hazel! Doesn't the wind sound like music today?"

SCENE 19 (4:30): Hazel pauses mid-dance to gently touch a dewdrop resting on a leaf, watching it sparkle with delight, soft magical light: "I love to dance, I love to sing, and I love finding beauty in the smallest things — a dewdrop, a leaf falling just right. This forest is full of it, if you just slow down and look."

SCENE 20 (4:45): Hazel spins one final graceful turn, arms open, flower petals swirling around her in the golden light, comes to a gentle stop facing camera with a warm smile: "Come dance along with us — there's still so much forest left to see."

SCENE 21 (5:00): Milo the hedgehog fidgets nervously in place, small spiky brown hedgehog, glancing around anxiously before straightening up with effort, soft forest light: "M-Milo. Hedgehog. I like rules, I like plans, and I like knowing exactly what's going to happen before it happens."

SCENE 22 (5:15): Milo curls slightly into a small worried ball for a moment, then slowly uncurls, taking a deep breath, standing a bit taller with a small proud smile: "Which, living with this group, almost never happens. But... I wouldn't trade them for anything. Probably. Okay — definitely."

SCENE 23 (5:30): Milo checks over his shoulder nervously, then musters courage and takes a small determined step forward toward camera, warm encouraging light: "Okay. Okay, let's keep going. One more friend to meet — the fast one. Very fast."

SCENE 24 (5:45): Pip the squirrel zips rapidly across tree branches in quick darting movements, skids to a sudden stop right in front of camera, wide excited grin, tail flicking fast, energetic motion, speaking rapidly: "HiI'mPip! Squirrel! I collect acorns, I climb literally everything, and I already know what everyone else just said because I was listening while also doing three other things!"

SCENE 25 (6:00): Pip darts up a tree trunk in a spiral, grabs an acorn mid-motion, leaps to the next branch without slowing down, talking the whole time: "I know every shortcut in this whole forest — this way's faster, that way's faster, honestly most ways are faster if you just commit—"

SCENE 26 (6:15): Pip suddenly freezes mid-motion, realizing something, grins and points offscreen toward the group, warm light: "Wait that's everybody! Okay bye — I mean hi — I mean, let's go find the others!"

SCENE 27 (6:30): Wide golden-hour shot: all eight characters — Coco, Oliver, Benny, Bruno, Luna, Hazel, Milo, Pip — standing together in the sunny forest clearing, warm and united, looking out at the forest together, soft warm sunset light. Narrator: "Eight friends, one magical forest, and more adventures than anyone could count. Some days they'll chase butterflies. Some days they'll get a little lost. But they always find their way — together."

SCENE 28 (6:45): Coco steps forward from the group toward camera, big warm smile, waving, the other seven friends visible behind her still waving, golden sunset light, camera slowly pulls back to reveal the whole forest: "There's always something new happening here in Whisperwood Forest — come back soon and see what we get up to next!"

SCENE 29 (7:00): Coco stands facing the camera directly in a warm, sunny spot, tail wagging happily, big friendly smile, warm inviting light: "If you had fun with us today, ask a grown-up to help you hit that subscribe button, so you never miss our next adventure here in Whisperwood Forest!"

Continuous score arc: warm/curious for scenes 1-2, light and playful under Coco/Pip/Benny sections, calmer and warmer under Oliver/Luna/Hazel sections, swelling gently for the group finale (scenes 26-27), staying warm and bright through the close and subscribe CTA (28-29).
