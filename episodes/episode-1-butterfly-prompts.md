# Episode 1 — "Coco and the Little Butterfly"

14 clips × 15 seconds = ~3.5 minutes. Model: Seedance 2.0. Aspect ratio 16:9.
Moral: *some beautiful things are meant to stay free.*

Same workflow as Episode 0: generate every clip with **`generate_audio: true`** — Seedance's
native ambience is worth keeping, its own guess at the dialogue voice doesn't matter, since
that gets replaced. Use the **video_prompt (lip-sync)** version below for any clip where a
character speaks on screen — it has the actual line baked in so the mouth movement matches
the words. The **lines** section lists the locked voice per character for the audio/dub pass
that happens at final assembly, same as Episode 0.
