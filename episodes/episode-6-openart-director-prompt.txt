WHISPERWOOD FOREST — EPISODE 6: "THE BERRY SECRET" ("A Bogyós Titok")
Pixar/Disney 3D animation style throughout, 16:9, warm consistent color palette. Characters: Bruno (large brown bear), Coco (curious fox, series lead), Luna (gray wolf, intro only). Lesson: good things don't get smaller when you share them — they get better. No scares, no real danger, ages 3-7 audience.

SCENE 1 (intro, pre-roll): Luna stands alert and steady in a sunny spot at the edge of Whisperwood Forest, facing the camera directly, calm and confident, speaking briefly: "Hey. It's Luna. Today's story is 'The Berry Secret.' Trust me — you'll want to watch this one."

SCENE 2 (0:00): Establishing shot of Whisperwood Forest in soft morning light, mist still clinging low between the trees. Camera drifts toward a dense, tangled thicket at the edge of the frame, sunlight catching on dew. No characters visible yet. Narrator: "Some mornings in Whisperwood Forest hide a little secret — if you know exactly where to look."

SCENE 3 (0:15): Bruno pushes through thick bushes and freezes mid-step, eyes going wide with delight at a hidden clearing overflowing with plump, glistening berries catching the morning light, speaking: "Whoa... whoa whoa whoa. Would you look at THAT."

SCENE 4 (0:30): Bruno glances over both shoulders, checking no one is watching, then carefully drags branches and leaves across the narrow gap in the bushes to hide the entrance, muttering to himself: "If I tell anybody about this, there won't be enough left for me. Better keep this one just... mine."

SCENE 5 (0:45): Inside the hidden clearing, Bruno sits alone surrounded by berries, eating happily at first, berry juice on his muzzle — but as the light shifts from morning to midday, his chewing slows, his eyes drifting toward the empty space beside him. Narrator: "So Bruno feasted, and feasted, and feasted some more. It was delicious. It was also... a little quiet."

SCENE 6 (1:00): Coco bounds through the forest, then stops abruptly, nose twitching, sniffing the air with growing excitement, speaking: "Ooh — what IS that smell? That is a very, very good smell."

SCENE 7 (1:15): Coco follows her nose through the forest, weaving between trees and ducking under branches, getting closer and closer to the hidden thicket, tail wagging with anticipation. No dialogue.

SCENE 8 (1:30): Coco pushes through the leaves at the edge of the clearing and freezes, spotting Bruno mid-bite, surrounded by berries. Both go still, staring at each other. Coco, with wide-eyed surprise: "Bruno?! You found a WHOLE secret berry patch and didn't tell anyone?"

SCENE 9 (1:45): Bruno freezes, a berry still in one paw, ears drooping with guilt, speaking slowly: "I— okay, yes. I found it yesterday. I didn't want it to run out."

SCENE 10 (2:00): Coco tilts her head, studying Bruno, more thoughtful than angry, speaking gently: "But... eating all these amazing berries all by yourself? That sounds kind of lonely, actually."

SCENE 11 (2:15): Bruno looks down at the berry in his paw, then up at Coco, something shifting in his expression, speaking quietly: "Huh. I guess... I didn't think about that part."

SCENE 12 (2:30): Bruno picks the biggest, juiciest berry from the bush and holds it out toward Coco, speaking warmly: "Here. Try this one — it's the best one I've found all day."

SCENE 13 (2:45): Bruno and Coco sit together in the berry clearing, eating and laughing, berry juice on both their faces, warm golden light. Bruno, pausing mid-laugh: "Huh... this is way better with someone to share it with."

SCENE 14 (3:00): Wide, warm shot of the berry clearing in golden afternoon light. Bruno and Coco sit together, content and full, surrounded by berries that seem to gleam even brighter now. Narrator: "Bruno learned something sweet that day: good things don't get smaller when you share them. Somehow, they just get better."

SCENE 15 (3:15): Bruno turns to face the camera, warm and happy, berry-stained smile, speaking: "Oh, and don't worry — of course we're telling everyone else about the berries too. There's always something new happening here in Whisperwood Forest — come back soon and see what we get up to next!" Coco visible beside him, smiling proudly.

SCENE 16 (3:30): Coco stands facing the camera directly in a warm, sunny spot, tail wagging happily, big friendly smile, warm inviting light: "If you had fun with us today, ask a grown-up to help you hit that subscribe button, so you never miss our next adventure here in Whisperwood Forest!"

Continuous score arc: playful and curious for the discovery (scenes 2-4), warm but quietly wistful during the solo feast (5), light and eager as Coco follows the scent (6-7), a little awkward and tense through the discovery and confession (8-11), warm and joyful once they share and laugh together (12-13), settled and content for the resolution (14), then bright and warm through the close and subscribe CTA (15-16).
