WHISPERWOOD FOREST — EPISODE 1: "COCO AND THE LITTLE BUTTERFLY"
Pixar/Disney 3D animation style throughout, 16:9, warm consistent color palette. Characters: Coco (fox, series lead), Oliver (owl, wise mentor). Moral: some beautiful things are meant to stay free. No scares, no real danger, ages 3-7 audience. Ambient forest sound throughout, no background music unless noted.

SCENE 1 (0:00): Establishing shot of Whisperwood Forest at golden morning hour, sunlight through leaves, colorful wildflowers, sparkling stream, birds and butterflies drifting. Camera glides forward and finds Coco trotting happily into frame, sniffing at flowers, tail wagging, big amber eyes full of wonder. Narrator: "Deep in the heart of the forest, where the sunlight dances through the leaves and the stream sings its gentle song... lies a very special place. This is Whisperwood Forest. And today, something wonderful is about to happen."

SCENE 2 (0:15): Coco suddenly freezes mid-step, eyes going WIDE with amazement — a vivid electric-blue butterfly rests on a yellow flower nearby, wings slowly opening and closing, shimmering in golden light. Close-up on Coco's face, pure wonder and longing, reaching one paw toward it: "Oh... oh my. You are the most beautiful thing I have ever seen in my whole entire life. I'm going to catch you. And then you can be mine forever!"

SCENE 3 (0:30): The blue butterfly lifts off and flutters away. Coco immediately chases after it through the forest, running fast, ears back, tail streaming behind her, leaping over a small sparkling stream, mid-leap calling out: "Almost got you — whoa!" — shakes her head with water flying off her ears, keeps running. Narrator over the action: "And so the chase began! The butterfly fluttered from flower to flower, and Coco followed — jumping, running, and tumbling through the forest."

SCENE 4 (0:45): Coco continues chasing through the forest, crawls under a leafy bush (a leaf stuck comically on her head), pops out speaking toward the butterfly: "I can do this. I just need to be a little bit faster... Wait! Please — just — wait for me!" — scrambles over a big mossy fallen log, sliding down with a thud, panting heavily, warm forest light.

SCENE 5 (1:00): Coco stops running, panting, looks up at Oliver perched calmly on a branch above her, watching with kind, curious, gently amused eyes over his round glasses, dappled afternoon light. Oliver: "Good afternoon, Coco. You seem to be in quite a hurry today." Coco, panting: "Oliver! I've been chasing that butterfly and I can't catch it and it's SO beautiful and I just want to keep it forever — have you seen which way it went?"

SCENE 6 (1:15): Oliver tilts his head thoughtfully, still perched: "Hm. I did see it, yes. But tell me something first, little one. Why do you want to catch it?" Coco, sitting below him, listening intently: "Because it's beautiful! I want to look at it every single day. I want it to be mine." Warm afternoon forest light.

SCENE 7 (1:30): Oliver, from his branch: "And do you think... that a butterfly is happy, sitting still in one place? Never flying? Never dancing on the wind?" Close-up on Coco's thoughtful face: "I... I never thought about that." Oliver: "Come. I want to show you something." Soft bokeh forest background, warm dappled light.

SCENE 8 (1:45): Oliver glides down from his branch and walks alongside Coco as they head deeper into the forest together, side by side, Coco uncertain but trusting, glancing up at him occasionally, warm afternoon light growing brighter as they approach a clearing ahead. Narrator: "Coco wasn't so sure about that. But she trusted Oliver. She always did."

SCENE 9 (2:00): Oliver and Coco step out of the forest edge together into a breathtaking open meadow, warm golden afternoon sun, dozens of vivid colorful butterflies flying freely over a sea of wildflowers. Coco, eyes filling with overwhelming wonder: "Oh... oh wow." Oliver, beside her: "This is where they come to be free, Coco. Every single one of them. Not one, not yours — all of them, together."

SCENE 10 (2:15): Wide cinematic shot of the magical meadow, dozens of beautiful butterflies dancing freely over lush colorful flowers in warm golden light, camera slowly pans across the scene. Oliver: "Isn't that more beautiful than one butterfly in a box?" Coco, small in the foreground, watching with quiet awe, nodding: "It's the most beautiful thing I've ever seen."

SCENE 11 (2:30): Coco stands very still in the meadow. The same electric-blue butterfly drifts gently toward her and lands softly on the very tip of her nose. Coco goes completely still, eyes crossing slightly trying to see it, the butterfly slowly opening and closing its wings, a long quiet magical moment. Narrator: "And then — something magical happened."

SCENE 12 (2:45): The blue butterfly gently lifts off Coco's nose and floats freely away into the golden light over the meadow. Coco's eyes follow it, glistening, a soft smile forming: "It chose me. It came to me on its own." Oliver, watching nearby with a warm knowing expression: "Sometimes, the most beautiful things find us — when we stop trying to hold on to them."

SCENE 13 (3:00): Golden sunset. Coco sits peacefully at the edge of the meadow, silhouetted against a warm pink and orange sky, butterflies still drifting freely around her, calm and content. Narrator: "That day, Coco learned something she would never forget. Some beautiful things are meant to stay free. And when we let them be free... they share their magic with everyone."

SCENE 14 (3:15): Coco turns to face the camera directly, warm happy smile, still in the golden sunset meadow, Oliver visible in the soft background watching fondly, camera slowly pulls back to reveal the whole magical meadow: "I'm really glad that butterfly found me instead. There's always something new happening here in Whisperwood Forest — come back soon and see what we get up to next!"

SCENE 15 (3:30): Coco stands facing the camera directly in a warm, sunny spot, tail wagging happily, big friendly smile, warm inviting light: "If you had fun with us today, ask a grown-up to help you hit that subscribe button, so you never miss our next adventure here in Whisperwood Forest!"

Continuous score arc: gentle/curious (scenes 1-2), playful and upbeat during the chase (3-4), calm and warm for the Oliver dialogue (5-8), swelling with wonder at the meadow (9-10), dropping to near-silence for the nose-landing moment (11), then tender and warm through the close (12-14), staying warm and bright through the subscribe CTA (15).
