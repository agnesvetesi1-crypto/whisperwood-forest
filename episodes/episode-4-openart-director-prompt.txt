WHISPERWOOD FOREST — EPISODE 4: "LUNA NEVER STOPS" ("Luna Sosem Áll Meg")
Pixar/Disney 3D animation style throughout, 16:9, warm consistent color palette. Characters: Luna (gray wolf, protective and watchful), Hazel (tan deer, graceful and artistic). Lesson: whoever always looks after everyone else deserves to be looked after too. No scares, no real danger, ages 3-7 audience.

SCENE 1 (0:00): Establishing shot of Whisperwood Forest in warm late-afternoon light. Camera drifts toward a lively clearing in the distance — faint sounds of laughter and playful chatter drift on the breeze, wildflowers swaying. No characters visible yet. Narrator: "Some afternoons in Whisperwood Forest are just for playing. But even on the happiest days... someone is always keeping watch."

SCENE 2 (0:15): Luna stands at the edge of a sunlit clearing, alert and still, ears turned outward toward the forest rather than toward the clearing behind her. Behind her, soft out-of-focus movement and color hints at friends laughing and playing in the meadow. Luna's posture is steady, watchful, apart from the fun. Narrator: "Luna always stood a little apart. Watching the paths. Listening to the wind. Just in case."

SCENE 3 (0:30): Hazel pauses mid-step in the sunny clearing, a half-finished flower crown in her hooves, glancing back over her shoulder toward Luna at the tree line, head tilted thoughtfully, speaking softly to herself: "She's not dancing again. She never dances."

SCENE 4 (0:45): Hazel crosses the clearing and comes to stand beside Luna at the tree line, offering a warm gentle smile: "Come dance with us, Luna. Just for a little while?" Luna, glancing at Hazel then back out at the forest: "Someone has to keep watch, Hazel. That's just... what I do."

SCENE 5 (1:00): Hazel studies Luna's face for a moment, gentle and curious, speaking quietly: "But who keeps watch over you?" Luna blinks, caught off guard, clearly not having an answer.

SCENE 6 (1:15): Luna looks away, a little uncomfortable, then speaks: "I don't need watching. I'm fine right here." She turns her gaze firmly back out toward the trees.

SCENE 7 (1:30): Hazel sits a little apart, carefully weaving wildflowers and soft leaves together into a small crown, glancing occasionally toward Luna with a thoughtful, fond expression, warm patient mood. Narrator: "So Hazel didn't argue. She just... started making something."

SCENE 8 (1:45): Hazel walks back to Luna and gently places a delicate flower crown over her ears, stepping back to admire it, speaking warmly: "There. Now you sit, and let me watch for a while. Just for five minutes."

SCENE 9 (2:00): Luna touches the flower crown gently with one paw, unsure, glancing between the crown and the forest, speaking: "I don't know how to just... sit."

SCENE 10 (2:15): Hazel sits down beside Luna, settling in close, speaking gently: "Then just sit badly. Nothing's going to happen in five minutes, Luna. I promise." Luna looks at Hazel, a small uncertain smile forming.

SCENE 11 (2:30): Luna slowly lowers herself down onto the soft grass beside Hazel, shoulders loosening for the first time, speaking quietly: "It's hard. Always watching. I forget how to stop." Hazel listens warmly.

SCENE 12 (2:45): Hazel leans her head gently against Luna's shoulder, speaking softly: "You don't have to stop forever. Just for right now." Luna closes her eyes for a moment, soaking in the quiet.

SCENE 13 (3:00): Luna and Hazel sit together in the golden meadow as the sun begins to set, the flower crown still resting on Luna's head, both watching the sky in comfortable silence. Narrator: "That day, Luna learned something new: watching over everyone else didn't mean she couldn't be watched over too. And for five whole minutes, she let herself just... be."

SCENE 14 (3:15): Luna turns to face the camera, warm and a little surprised at her own smile, still wearing the flower crown: "There's always something new happening here in Whisperwood Forest — come back soon and see what we get up to next!" Hazel visible beside her, smiling proudly.

SCENE 15 (3:30): Coco stands facing the camera directly in a warm, sunny spot, tail wagging happily, big friendly smile, warm inviting light: "If you had fun with us today, ask a grown-up to help you hit that subscribe button, so you never miss our next adventure here in Whisperwood Forest!"
